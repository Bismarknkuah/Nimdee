# Platform owner guide

Log in at `/platform/login` with a platform account (the first one is created from `PLATFORM_ADMIN_EMAIL` / `PLATFORM_ADMIN_PASSWORD`).

* **Overview** — schools by status and plan, students, users, subscription revenue, sync health, recent registrations and pending approvals.
* **Schools** — search and filter; open a school to approve/reject a registration, suspend or reactivate, edit the subscription (plan, status, cycle, period end), issue subscription invoices and mark them paid (which applies the requested plan), grant **feature overrides**, download the school's **data export**, view its domains and recent audit trail.
* **Support session** — with a reason, mint a short-lived token that acts as the school administrator; the session is audited on both the platform and the school.
* **Plans** — prices (monthly/yearly), student limits, trial days and the feature list per plan.
* **Subscription invoices** — pending payments across all schools; marking paid activates/upgrades the school.
* **Sync health** — offline devices, pending operations, failed operations and conflicts per school.
* **Platform users** — owners and support staff.
* **Audit log** — every audited action across all schools, including support sessions and data exports.
