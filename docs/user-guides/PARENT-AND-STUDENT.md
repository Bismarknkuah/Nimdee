# Parent & student guide

## Signing in

Use the email the school registered and the temporary password they gave you; you will be asked to change it. Parents see all their children; students see themselves.

## Parent home

* A card per child: attendance rate (30 days), fees balance, canteen wallet balance, latest published result and the class teacher's contact.
* **Homework due** across all children, **upcoming events**, **announcements**, and a badge for unread **messages**.

## Child page tabs

* **Attendance** — counts by status and the daily history.
* **Results** — every published term with subject scores, grades, positions and teacher comments; download the report card PDF.
* **Fees** — invoices, payments and receipts; **Pay online** via Paystack (Mobile Money or card) if the school has enabled it; receipts are issued automatically.
* **Timetable** — the weekly lessons.
* **Homework** — instructions, due dates, scores and feedback; *Mark done* tells the teacher the work was handed in.
* **Canteen wallet** — balance, daily limit, transactions; **Top up online**.
* **Behaviour** — incidents recorded by the school, with actions taken and points.
* **Health** — allergies, conditions and clinic visits recorded by the school nurse.
* **Transport** — bus route, driver contact, stop and pick-up/drop-off times.
* **Library** — books borrowed, due dates and fines.
* **Events** — upcoming school and class events.

## Messages

**Messages → New conversation** to write to any of your child's teachers or the school office. Replies arrive as notifications.

## Student home

Today's lessons, homework pending, latest average, attendance rate, canteen wallet, events and announcements, with quick links to results, attendance, library and messages.
