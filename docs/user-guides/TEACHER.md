# Teacher guide

Your dashboard lists your classes, today's lessons, homework to grade, open behaviour incidents, unread messages and upcoming events, with a reminder if attendance has not been marked today.

## Attendance (works offline)

1. Open **Attendance**, pick the class and date. Tap P/A/L/E/S for each student, or *All present* then adjust; add notes if needed. *Save attendance*.
2. Before going to an area without internet, press **Download for offline**. Marks saved offline are kept on the device and synced automatically when you reconnect (a pill in the header shows pending items; tap it to sync now).
3. **Reports** shows each student's rate for a date range and flags those below the school minimum.

## Homework & assignments

**Assignments → New assignment**: class, subject, title, instructions, due date, optional max score. Publishing notifies parents and students. Open an assignment to see who has submitted, mark late/missing, and enter scores and feedback.

## Marks & results

**Results & assessments**: create class tests, projects and the exam per subject (with max score and weight), enter marks (Enter moves to the next student), then **Compute results**. Result sheets show average, grade, position and attendance; add class-teacher remarks, then **Submit for review**. Report cards (PDF) are available per sheet and per class.

## Behaviour

**Discipline → Log incident**: student, category, severity, description, action taken, demerit points, and whether to notify the parent. You can only log incidents for your own classes.

## Messages & calendar

**Messages** lets you write to parents of your classes and to colleagues, or start a thread with all parents of a class. **Calendar & events** shows school and class events.

## Leave

**HR & payroll → Request leave** (type, dates, reason). Approvals appear on your dashboard; payslips can be opened as PDF once payroll is marked paid.

## Printables

From a class page: class list, attendance register and ID card sheet as PDF. From the timetable: your weekly timetable as PDF.
