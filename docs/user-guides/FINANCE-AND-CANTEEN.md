# Finance & canteen guide

## Accountant / cashier

The **finance dashboard** shows invoiced vs collected, outstanding by age (not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days), collections over 30 days, payment methods, top debtors and recent payments.

* **Fees → Fee structures**: amounts per category for a level or a specific class, per term or every term, day or boarding. **Discounts**: scholarships and one-off discounts per student (the sibling discount is automatic from the rules).
* **Generate invoices** for a term (optionally one class). Existing unpaid invoices can be regenerated; invoices with payments are never touched.
* **Record payment**: find the student, choose the invoice (or leave unallocated), amount, method (cash, Mobile Money, card, bank, cheque), reference; a receipt number is issued and the receipt PDF can be printed or emailed to the payer.
* **Reverse** a payment (with reason) from the payment page; the ledger keeps both entries.
* **Statements** (PDF) and **invoices** (PDF with installment plan and payment history) are on the student and invoice pages.
* **Reports → Finance** for ageing buckets, collections by month, billed by category and defaulters; CSV exports on the invoices and payments pages.
* **HR & payroll → Payroll**: create a monthly run (pre-filled from basic salaries), adjust allowances/deductions, approve, mark paid; payslips as PDF.

## Canteen manager

* **Menu & stock**: items with price, unit, opening stock and low-stock threshold; stock in / waste / corrections are logged.
* **Wallets**: top up a student's wallet (cash/MoMo/bank), set a daily limit, freeze a wallet.
* **Point of sale**: tap items, pick the student (search or scan the ID card QR), charge the wallet (limits and balance enforced) or take cash; low stock and daily sales are on the canteen dashboard.
* Parents can top up online from the portal; every wallet movement is visible to them.
